Domus

Informational carousel posts for Instagram

  • 🎨 Role: Graphic design, diseño para redes sociales
  • 🛠 Tools: Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, Infinite Painter.

The challenge

The main challenge was to communicate the main message while always maintaining the fun but calm aesthetic of Domus, which represents warmth, union and friendship.

Designing process

    In order to understand the needs of the client, two interviews were conducted prior to the beginning of the ideation to understand the main values of Domus and the main objective of the client.

With the findings of the interview a moodboard and exploration of the company’s branding was made, choosing the most characteristic elements, colors and types of illustrations.
