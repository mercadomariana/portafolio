Domus

Design and front-end development of a responsive website.

  • 🎨Role: Product design (UX/UI), front-end web development.
  • 🛠 Tools: Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap.

The website

The main objective of the website was to preserve and convey the essence of Domus, creating a digital experience that remains consistent with its identity. The site allows users to explore the services, get in touch easily, and access downloadable resources.

The design is fully responsive, ensuring optimal navigation on any device, and includes a form system that sends inquiries directly to the designated email address.

Desktop

  • Web with 5 sections

  • 100% responsive for every devide

  • Functional forms

Results

This project allowed Domus to establish a solid and coherent brand identity, fully reflected in its new website. The design unifies the brand's visual and communication elements, reinforcing its personality and purpose.

In addition, the platform enables closer and more direct contact with each family, creating an efficient and accessible communication bridge.

The website brings together all essential information about Domus's services, philosophy, and free resources in a single place, becoming a central reference point for users who want to learn about or connect with the organization.
