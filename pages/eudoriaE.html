Eudoria

Multi-sensory electronic part

  • 🎨Role: Graphic design, electronics
  • 🛠 Tools: Arduino, Canva

*Note: Group concept project

Creating the universe

In the ancient world, full of fights and conflicts over territory, came a world war that destroyed much of the territory of planet earth, and a lot of human life. The few survivors board a raft and sail aimlessly until they come across an island whose inhabitants have very different physiological characteristics and technological systems never seen before. They use huge tubes handled with air to transport, the kitchens automated to make it easier to make food. They are holistic beings, interested in personal growth, so they usually gather to meditate and always carry their sacred stones to absorb the bad energies. These beings accepted the surviving humans, on condition that they eliminate what led to their destruction, learn to live without territorial limits, religious, ethnic, etc.

Designing process

    The problem of migration was selected to base our universe on what a world would be like where moving anywhere is legal and without any restrictions.

Throughout history the moodboard was created to allow us to determine the traditions, types of food, cultural events and in general, the daily life of the inhabitants of Eudoria.

What's the piece about?

The piece represents an egg of the Eudorians to which the sacred crystals must be placed so that it can turn on its light and thus warm up the fetus so that it can grow healthy and strong.

Final result

There was a public exhibition at the arts faculty of the National University of La Plata in which people could participate in the interaction and immersion of the world of Eudoria.
